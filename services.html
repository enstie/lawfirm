Immigration Law Services

At Crest Immigration Law Firm, we offer comprehensive legal services to help you navigate the complex immigration system. Our experienced team is dedicated to providing personalized solutions for your unique situation.

Visa Applications

We provide expert assistance with all types of visa applications, ensuring your application is complete, accurate, and submitted on time.

  • Work Visas: H-1B, L-1, O-1, and other employment-based visas
  • Student Visas: F-1, M-1, and J-1 visas for educational purposes
  • Family-Based Visas: Spouse visas, fiancé(e) visas, and other family reunification options
  • Tourist/Visitor Visas: B-1/B-2 visas for temporary visits
  • Investor Visas: E-2 and EB-5 visas for business investors

Asylum & Refugee Protection

We understand the urgency and sensitivity of asylum cases. Our team provides compassionate, thorough representation for those seeking protection.

  • Asylum application preparation and filing
  • Refugee status assistance
  • Withholding of removal cases
  • Convention Against Torture (CAT) claims
  • Defensive asylum in removal proceedings

Citizenship & Naturalization

Becoming a citizen is a significant milestone. We guide you through every step of the naturalization process.

  • Eligibility assessment and consultation
  • Form N-400 preparation and filing
  • Interview preparation and representation
  • Citizenship test preparation support
  • Oath ceremony guidance
  • Derivative citizenship for children

Green Card Applications

Permanent residency is a critical step toward building your future. We handle all aspects of green card applications.

  • Employment-based green cards (EB-1, EB-2, EB-3)
  • Family-based green cards
  • Green card renewal and replacement
  • Adjustment of status applications
  • Consular processing

Deportation & Removal Defense

If you are facing deportation or removal proceedings, we provide aggressive defense to protect your rights and keep you with your family.

  • Representation in immigration court
  • Cancellation of removal applications
  • Appeals to the Board of Immigration Appeals (BIA)
  • Waivers of inadmissibility
  • Bond hearings

Business Immigration

We help businesses navigate immigration law to hire and retain international talent.

  • PERM labor certification
  • H-1B specialty occupation visas
  • L-1 intracompany transfer visas
  • E-2 treaty investor visas
  • Compliance and I-9 audits

Family Immigration

We help families stay together by providing expert guidance on family-based immigration petitions.

  • Spousal petitions (I-130)
  • Fiancé(e) visas (K-1)
  • Parent and sibling petitions
  • Adoption immigration
  • VAWA (Violence Against Women Act) petitions
